The silverback mesh and source PBR maps are Grab3D's [Silverback Gorilla](https://grab3d.com/models/animals/gorilla/), light GLB, released under CC0-1.0. The embedded source metadata identifies Grab3D and the license. This edition reduces the mesh to 81,074 triangles, adds twelve weighted bones with inverse bind matrices, and adapts the coat and athletic top with the official AtlasFit symbol. The panoramic gym lighting and AtlasFit wordmark reuse the local assets credited in public/forge/SOURCES.txt.
